1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled in connection with Picture Puzzle (the “App”), a mobile puzzle game for Android devices published on the Google Play Store by epallab (“we”, “us”, or “our”). epallab is an independent app developer. The App is built with the Unity game engine.

Picture Puzzle is a single-player game that runs on your device. It does not require an account, a login, or an internet connection to play. It contains no in-app purchases. The App is supported by advertising, which is delivered by Google AdMob and requires an internet connection to display.

This policy applies only to the Picture Puzzle Android application. It does not apply to the Google Play Store, to advertisers whose ads may appear in the App, or to any website or service you may reach by tapping an ad. Those are governed by their own privacy policies.

By installing and using Picture Puzzle, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with them, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Collect

epallab does not collect, store, or receive any personally identifiable information from you directly. We operate no servers, no user accounts, and no databases of player information.

We never ask you for your name, email address, phone number, postal address, date of birth, photographs, contacts, or payment details, and the App contains no forms or sign-up screens that could capture them. Your game progress, settings, and scores are stored locally on your own device and are not transmitted to us. Uninstalling the App removes that local data.

Information is, however, collected automatically by our third-party advertising partner, Google AdMob, when advertisements are requested and displayed in the App. This collection happens between your device and Google’s systems; the data goes to Google, not to us. Section 3 lists what is collected and Section 4 explains Google’s role.

Separately, if you choose to email us using the address in Section 15, we will necessarily see your email address and whatever you write. We use that only to respond to you and do not add it to any mailing list.

3. Automatically Collected Data

To request, display, and measure advertisements, the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) software development kit integrated into the App may collect and transmit the following categories of data to Google:

Android Advertising ID (AAID)
A resettable, pseudonymous identifier provided by Google Play services that is used to recognise your device for advertising purposes. It is not your name and is not permanently tied to you — you can reset or delete it at any time (see Section 7).
IP address and approximate (coarse) location
Your device’s IP address is visible whenever it connects to Google’s ad servers, and a coarse location — typically at the level of a city, region, or country — may be derived from it. The App does not request Android’s precise GPS location permission and does not access your exact location.
Device and technical characteristics
Device model and manufacturer, Android operating system version, device language and locale settings, screen size, resolution and orientation, network connection type, and similar technical attributes used to select and correctly render an ad.
Ad interaction data
Which ads were shown to you (impressions), whether you tapped them (clicks), how long an ad was viewed, whether a rewarded video was watched to completion, and whether an ad was closed or skipped.
App usage events related to ad serving
Limited in-app events tied to advertising, such as the App being opened or resumed, the point at which an ad request is made, which ad placement was involved (banner, interstitial, or rewarded video), and the version of the App and the ads SDK in use.

This data is collected by Google in its capacity as our advertising provider. We receive access only to aggregated, non-identifying reporting — for example, total impressions, total clicks, and estimated earnings for a given day or country. We cannot see, and are not given, the data of any individual user.

4. Third-Party Services

Google AdMob is the only third-party service integrated into Picture Puzzle that collects data. The App contains no social media SDKs, no separate analytics platform, no crash reporting service, and no advertising networks other than those Google works with to fill ad inventory.

Google AdMob

AdMob is Google’s mobile advertising platform. We use it to show banner ads, interstitial (full-screen) ads, and rewarded video ads inside the App, and this advertising revenue is what allows us to offer Picture Puzzle free of charge. When an ad slot needs to be filled, the AdMob SDK on your device sends a request to Google containing the data described in Section 3, and Google selects and returns an advertisement.

In doing so, Google acts as an independent data controller for the data it collects, and its own privacy policy and terms govern that processing. Google may also share data with, or receive data from, the third-party advertising partners and ad exchanges it works with in order to fill ad inventory. We encourage you to review Google’s documentation:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • AdMob advertising partners list

5. How We Use Information

Because we do not receive your personal data, the uses below describe what the data collected through AdMob is used for by Google on our behalf:

  • Ad delivery. Selecting and serving banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads that are technically compatible with your device, network, and language.
  • Ad personalization. Where you have given consent, showing ads that Google considers more relevant to you based on your advertising identifier and inferred interests. Without consent, ads are served on a non-personalized basis, using contextual signals rather than your interest profile.
  • Frequency capping. Limiting how often the same ad is repeated to you and spacing out full-screen ads so the game stays playable.
  • Fraud and abuse prevention. Detecting invalid traffic, automated clicks, and other abusive or fraudulent activity that would otherwise harm advertisers and the ad ecosystem.
  • Aggregate analytics and reporting. Producing summary statistics — impressions, click-through rates, fill rates, and estimated revenue — that help us understand how the App performs overall and how to improve it. These reports are aggregated and do not identify individual users.

We do not sell your personal information for money, we do not build profiles of players ourselves, and we do not use any of this data to contact you.
